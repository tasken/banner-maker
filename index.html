DS banner maker

Create custom icons and menu titles for Nintendo DS homebrew ROMs, and cover art for Pico Launcher.

Icon specs

DS icons are 32 × 32 px with only 16 colors, and the first color is transparent. This tool shrinks any image and reduces its colors for you, but starting with a matching image gives sharper results. A 32 × 32 PNG with a 16-color palette is used as-is.

Transparency detected

Your icon has transparent pixels:
• Pixels under 50% opacity become transparent on the console.
• Pixels at 50% opacity or more become solid in their own color. Colors aren't mixed with a background, so edges don't get a light or dark halo.

Cover specs

Pico Launcher shows covers at 106 × 96 px with up to 256 colors. Drop any image and this tool makes a cover.bmp in the exact format Pico reads.

Put cover.bmp on your SD card in one of these places:
• Inside a folder, as cover.bmp: the cover for that folder.
• In /_pico/covers/user/, renamed to the game's file name plus .bmp, like myGame.nds.bmp.
• In /_pico/covers/nds/ or /_pico/covers/gba/, renamed to the game's 4-letter code from its ROM header, like ABCD.bmp.
